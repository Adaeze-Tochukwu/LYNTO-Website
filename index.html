How it Works

Three steps. No disruption to your current system.

Lynto doesn't replace your care management software. It adds an early-warning layer on top of it — simple for carers to use, and clear for managers to act on.

1

Carers Record Observations

At each visit, carers use Lynto's simple guided wizard on their phone to answer a short set of structured questions about what they've seen. No free-text guesswork, no training required. It takes under two minutes and works on any smartphone — even with poor signal.

2

Lynto Highlights Concerns

The system compares observations against safe thresholds and automatically flags anything unusual as Amber or Red. If a concern is flagged Red, Lynto doesn't just send a notification — it calls the on-call manager or coordinator directly, straight away.

3

Managers Review and Act

Managers receive instant alerts and open a clear escalation summary — who raised it, what was observed, and what needs to happen next. No digging through notes, no chasing carers for updates. Everything needed to make a decision is right there.
